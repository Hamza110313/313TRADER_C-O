## Brand & Style

This design system blends rigorous electrical engineering with high-velocity consumer commerce. The aesthetic communicates uncompromising technical reliability, power conversion efficiency, and modern industrial clarity. It steers clear of playful whimsy or heavy industrial grit, leaning into a sharp, luminous, and authoritative presentation.

### Aesthetic Principles
- **Engineering Clarity:** Clean, structured information density. Technical data (wattage, voltage, efficiency tiers, certifications) receives equal typographic refinement to consumer pricing.
- **Electric Precision:** Minimalist surface treatments punctuated by high-signal state indicators and luminous accents that signal power, current, and solar storage.
- **High-Velocity Commerce:** Interfaces prioritize clear scan paths, frictionless comparisons, legible price-per-watt metrics, and unambiguous transactional states.

## Colors

The palette leverages a deep cobalt anchor, energized by electric cyan and solar-derived amber highlights. Surface layers prioritize contrast and technical legibility.

### Surface & Canvas
- **Canvas Base:** `#F8FAFC` (Slate-50) establishes a crisp, glare-free foundation.
- **Surface Elevation:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White) defines interactive cards, sheets, and modular panels.
- **Borders & Dividers:** `#E2E8F0` provides crisp 1px structural separation without heavy visual weight. Subdued structural borders drop to `#F1F5F9`.

### Typography Colors
- **Text Primary:** `#0F172A` (Slate-900) delivers high-contrast readability for product titles, core metrics, and primary figures.
- **Text Secondary:** `#475569` (Slate-600) handles narrative specifications and supporting labels.
- **Text Muted:** `#64748B` (Slate-500) supports auxiliary metadata, SKUs, and unit notations.

### Semantic & Domain Badges
- **Eco / Solar Efficiency:** `#10B981` (Emerald-500) with `#ECFDF5` background tint.
- **Energy Saving / Solar Yield:** `#F59E0B` (Amber-500) with `#FFFBEB` background tint.
- **High-Demand / Critical Alert:** `#E11D48` (Rose-600) with `#FFF1F2` background tint.

## Typography

The type stack is unified around Plus Jakarta Sans to harmonize modern technical structure with commercial clarity.

### Usage Guidance
- **Pricing:** Always apply font-weight `800` for transaction-critical pricing. Currency symbols drop down 20% in size and sit flush with the top baseline.
- **Spec Labels:** Always render in full uppercase with a tracking of `0.04em` (`spec-label`) to ensure legibility when set adjacent to numerical data.
- **Spec Values:** Pair spec labels with semibold values (`spec-value`) using tabular numerals for vertical comparison across product sheets.

## Layout & Spacing

A strictly regimented 8px-based layout system governs component layout and column flow.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12-column layout, max-width `1440px`, centered with `2rem` outer margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Product grids conform strictly to 4-up or 3-up columns.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout, `1.5rem` outer margins, `1rem` gutters. Product grids flow 2-up or 3-up.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, `1rem` margins, `0.75rem` gutters. Product grids support single-column full cards or dense 2-column comparative browsing.

### Spacing Application
- Use `space-xs` (4px) for inline metadata gaps, unit-to-value locks, and badge internal padding.
- Use `space-sm` (8px) between icon and text pairs, and for stack gaps in spec grids.
- Use `space-md` (16px) for interior card padding and primary form control gaps.
- Use `space-lg` (24px) for card-to-card stacking and section subgroup headers.
- Use `space-xl` (40px) for macro section vertical division.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses crisp, low-contrast structural outlines paired with luminous, cool-tinted ambient shadows. Avoid heavy dropshadows that muddy engineering data.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Applied to the main `#F8FAFC` canvas. No shadow.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Resting):** `#FFFFFF` surfaces bounded by a continuous 1px `#E2E8F0` border. Ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Elevated Drawers):** Applied to active product cards, floating toolbars, and search dropdowns. 1px `#CBD5E1` border with shadow: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Controls & Overlays):** Sticky bottom navigation bars, quick-cart side sheets, and modal technical viewers. Shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a soft-edged industrial shape language (Level 1). Radii remain tight and deliberate to reflect technical hardware rather than casual social apps.

### Corner Radii Tokens
- **Core Elements (`rounded` - 0.25rem / 4px):** Product badges, technical data cells, checkboxes, standard buttons, and small input controls.
- **Containers (`rounded-lg` - 0.5rem / 8px):** Product cards, flyout menus, search modals, and spec table wrappers.
- **Panels & Drawers (`rounded-xl` - 0.75rem / 12px):** Cart side-drawers, primary alert hero banners, and checkout accordions.
- **Pill Exception (`rounded-full`):** Category filter chips and floating action buttons only.

## Components

### Product Cards
- **Base:** `#FFFFFF` background, 1px `#E2E8F0` border, `0.5rem` radius. Interior padding: `1rem`.
- **Badge Anchor:** Absolute top-left within the image container; displays solar/deal tags with `0.25rem` radius, `spec-label` uppercase typography, and tinted background.
- **Image Display:** Clean, neutral `#F8FAFC` image backing with a fixed 1:1 aspect ratio.
- **Pricing Lockup:** Prominent bold display with strikethrough original price and savings percentage in Rose-600.
- **Action Layer:** Primary CTA button ("Add to Cart" or quick-add icon) anchored to card bottom. Quick-spec metrics (e.g., "1200W Output | 98% Eff.") sit directly above price in a muted two-column micro-grid.

### Buttons & Quick Actions
- **Primary:** Deep Cobalt `#0369A1` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `0.25rem` radius, height `40px` (desktop) or `48px` (mobile touch target). Hover shifts to `#075985`. Focus ring: 2px `#0284C7` offset by 2px white.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background, 1px `#E2E8F0` outline, `#0F172A` text. Hover shifts to `#F1F5F9`.
- **Quick-Add Cart Button:** Square `40px x 40px` button or full-width low-height bar with a shopping bag/lightning-bolt icon.

### Form Inputs & Search Header
- **Search Header:** Integrated search bar with 1px `#CBD5E1` border, `#F8FAFC` background shifting to `#FFFFFF` on focus, paired with category selector pills directly below.
- **Inputs:** Height `40px`, padding `0 0.75rem`, `0.25rem` radius. Label positioned above in `spec-label` style. Error states introduce a 1px border in `#E11D48` and an inline message.

### Filter & Category Chips
- **Resting:** `#FFFFFF` background, 1px `#E2E8F0` border, `#475569` text, `rounded-full` geometry.
- **Selected:** `#0369A1` background, `#FFFFFF` text, border matches background. Counter count inside chip rendered in semi-transparent white.

### Technical Specification Tables
- **Structure:** Alternating row striping (`#FFFFFF` and `#F8FAFC`), bounded by a subtle `#E2E8F0` exterior border.
- **Cells:** Left column renders `spec-label` in `#64748B`, right column renders `spec-value` in `#0F172A` with tabular numerical alignment. Row height: strictly `36px` to maintain high data density.

### Mobile Navigation & Header
- **Header:** Sticky `#FFFFFF` bar with crisp bottom border, containing brand mark, search icon button, and cart badge with numerical counter in Rose-600.
- **Bottom Navigation Bar:** Mobile-only sticky bottom bar containing 4 primary nodes: Catalog, Spec Search, Orders, and Sticky Cart Summary with real-time subtotal and checkout CTA.
